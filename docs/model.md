# Deterministic educational model 1.0.0
Shared implementation: lib/shared/model.ts. Backend computes authoritative scenario outputs; adviser energy uses the same function. Constant 2026 USD, t=0 decision/development, t=1..10 annual service intervals. No taxes, inflation, grants or salvage. Fixed 8% discount; distinct 6% loan interest. All demand, costs and performance equivalence are illustrative assumptions, not demonstrated demand or quotes.

Nameplate: facility MW=IT MW×PUE; full-load GWh=MW×8760/1000. 20×1.25=25 MW, 219 GWh. PUE1.4=28 MW,245.28 GWh. Operating power = installed MW×(.35+.65×productive utilization), multiplied by PUE and commissioned operating hours; idle power prevents halving electricity at half demand.

H100 SXM80GB reference, 1.4kW allocated system GPU, 10% other IT, floor remaining budget/1.4; 95% usable hours once. Baseline floor(18000/1.4)=12,857 GPUs; demand=12,857×.95×8760×.60=64,197,572.4 productive hours/year. Planning demand frozen across scale changes. Workload shares training60%,teaching25%,inference15% are uncommitted allocations. Small scale leases overflow; no unmet workload is used to manufacture a cheap option.

Construction USD8m/MW, grid upgrade .5m/MW, GPUs30k/unit, other IT1.5m/MW, staff180k/MW/year, facility maintenance2%/year, lease3/hour plus10% service overhead. IRL.15/FIN.10/CAN.10 USD/kWh are indicative assumptions, not offered tariffs. Other IT/storage/support/egress limitations explicit; equivalent H100 productive service remains a critical evidence gap.

Opening month24. Ten percent initial facility draw at t0, remaining facility/grid/equipment at commissioning bucket; fractional operating hours for half-year delays. This deliberately simplified draw convention is not a construction schedule. Debt60%, seven-year straight principal, annual interest on outstanding balance; early draw accrues carrying interest during delay. Economic cost=assets+operations+interest; principal repayment and debt draw shown separately, never added to capex. Funding equity cash before opening includes operations/interest; lender schedule is hypothetical. No reserve/lease minimum or non-cancellable commitment assumed. Capital-at-risk=owned capital committed through opening less zero recovery; lease-only exposure0 because service is assumed cancellable. Not expected loss.

Build full capacity after opening. Lease starts at decision date, no owned capital. Hybrid initially half, fixed expansion start year4 (month36), deferred until grid is available. GPU replacement at four-year tranche age, other IT at five-year age; expansion and replacements are separate. At end-of-year t10 replacement is charged by conservative annual convention with no salvage; this horizon effect can make delayed ownership look cheaper despite greater pre-opening cash. Do not interpret that artifact as a delay benefit.

Required cases: base; +12mo grid arrival with same workload/bridge/carrying; half productive demand, unchanged installed fleet/fixed costs, adjusted power. Gross opening cash, equity opening cash, year5 operating (with every yearly row), total/discounted cost, undiscounted USD/hour and separately discounted cost/hours, capital exposure, delivered/unused/overflow/unmet hours, modeled energy all returned. Unused cost=(1-realized productive ownership utilization)×fixed ownership allocation; already within totals, never additional cost.

Controls follow PRD ranges/steps; country separate. Non-GPU power budget nonnegative. Zero demand unit cost unavailable. Hash=SHA256 normalized scenario; design revision/evidence hash/model version attached to results. Canonical revisions persist exact inputs/evidence IDs. Historical snapshots remain in D1.

Recommendation gates: workload equivalence, site/grid and comparable quotes missing → INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE. Cost-only ranking is explicitly illustrative and cannot override gaps. Generic ranking code handles exact ties/invalid/missing costs. No preferred country seeded. The initial course outcome can be insufficient evidence; it is not investment approval. All seven one-at-a-time bounded sweeps run; no verdict reversal while evidence gate remains. Missing evidence gaps are different from cost sensitivity drivers.

Known limits: coarse uniform GPU equivalence/power curve; yearly replacement bucket and capex draw; no measured climate/water, uptime/fuel, site cost, member commitment, debt terms or current delivered lease quote. Ownership design/governance/delivery hypotheses appear on Initial Design; independent review required.
