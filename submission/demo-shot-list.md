# Two-minute demonstration script — current published application
Rehearse at about 125 words per minute. Sign in/register before recording; start with reset baseline. Hide account details. Do not promise an exact AI response. Pause recording for long waits if permitted by the instructor.

## 0:00–0:20 — Overview
“This is our Datacenter Design Explorer, built to help a university consortium compare building, leasing, and a phased hybrid across Ireland, Finland, and Canada. The baseline screening favors Finland with a hybrid strategy, but this is a conditional model result, not investment approval. The sensitivity analysis shows that plausible changes can reverse the strategy choice.”

## 0:20–0:45 — What-if inputs and required stress cases
“The starting design has 20 megawatts of IT load and PUE 1.25, giving 25 megawatts of facility power. Changing PUE to 1.4 and pressing Evaluate scenario gives 28 megawatts and 245.28 gigawatt-hours at full power for a year. This changes my scenario, not the shared baseline. We also compare the required base case, one-year grid delay, and half-utilization case, reporting opening cash, annual operating costs, cost per productive GPU-hour, and capital at risk.”
[Change PUE, evaluate, show cards and stress outputs, reset.]

## 0:45–1:05 — Country Comparison and Initial Design
“Country prices use published benchmarks with their dates and limitations. They are not supplier offers. Our initial design shows power, cooling, networking and storage, plus the largest-component failure and a 48-hour grid outage. Uptime remains unverified without site-specific engineering and fuel or backup arrangements.”

## 1:05–1:25 — Evidence
“Evidence separates facts, assumptions, calculations, design decisions and unknowns. Each source has a link and date. Authorized team members can add evidence or refresh World Bank data without rebuilding the site. If the external API fails, the system retains the last valid data.”
[Open one source link; show the refresh control if authorized. Do not trigger a failure during the short recording.]

## 1:25–1:50 — Ask the Adviser
“The adviser requires sign-in and registration. I’ll ask why the recommendation is sensitive. The backend retrieves D1 evidence and deterministic results, then validates the answer’s citations. The AI explains the model and missing evidence; it cannot approve construction or invent a utility commitment.”
[Ask “Why is the overall recommendation assumption-sensitive?” Show the actual answer and a citation.]

## 1:50–2:00 — Conclusion
“Our deliverables include the published site, diagrams, schema, sources and test results. The model supports further investigation; actual demand, workload performance and delivered site quotes still need verification.”
